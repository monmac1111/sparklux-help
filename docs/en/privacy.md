# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-22**

This policy describes how Sparklux (“the app”) handles personal information.

## 1. What is never collected

**The app does not collect or transmit any of the following.**

- The **content** of the videos you play
- **File names or paths**
- **Viewing history**
- **Contact details** such as your name, email address or phone number
- **Location**
- **Identifiers** used to track you across apps or devices

The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting.

## 2. Stored on your Mac only

The following is kept **on your Mac only** and is never transmitted.

| Information | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recent items and playback position | Local app storage | To resume where you left off |
| Display calibration values | Local app storage | To show brightness correctly |
| Display settings (preset, upscaling) | Local app storage | To remember your choices |

These are removed when you delete the app.

## 3. Stored in iCloud

**Only the start date of your free trial** is stored in your private iCloud database.

- The purpose is **managing the free trial** (so that reinstalling does not reset it)
- It is stored in **your own iCloud**, and **the developer cannot read it**
- It contains **no** video content, file names or viewing history
- The app works even if you are not signed in to iCloud or cannot reach it
  (in that case the trial is tracked on your Mac only)

## 4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by **Apple (the App Store)**. The app never receives or stores
payment details such as card numbers. Whether you have purchased is determined on your
Mac from the receipt Apple issues.

## 5. Network activity

The app communicates only in the following cases.

| When | What | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| You open a URL | A request for the address you entered | The destination you specified |
| Free-trial management | Reading and writing the start date | Apple iCloud |
| Purchase and restore | Receipt validation | Apple |

**No video-related network activity occurs unless you open a URL.**

### About the external tool (yt-dlp)

To play a streaming-service URL, the app invokes `yt-dlp` **that you installed yourself**.
The app neither bundles nor installs yt-dlp. Any network activity performed by yt-dlp is
governed by the policy of its distributor.

## 6. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13
(it collects no personal information from anyone).

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date revised.
Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
